Make a direct request when an introduction is what you want. A coffee chat is useful when you actually want a conversation. Shared interests can be a reason to stay in touch even if no job results.

**If immediate income is at risk:**

1. Contact the adviser or recruiter who helped through [Lewisham Works](https://lewisham.gov.uk/lewishamworks). Ask for two named, paid admin, data or reporting assignments with start dates. Consider established agencies or direct employers for shifts, and get the rate, hours, location and pay date in writing.
2. Test one small paid service with a few appropriate contacts: adult maths/statistics/Excel tutoring or fixing a defined report. Agree price and scope first. Do not treat a registration or a trial as booked income.
3. Ask the tutoring platform which check it needs, who arranges it, whether it accepts an overseas record and the total fees. [DBS guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/dbs-check-requests-guidance-for-employers) allows applications even while abroad, although UK DBS does not normally see records held overseas. [Eligible self-employed people](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/dbs-checks-for-self-employed-people-and-personal-employees) have been able to request enhanced checks through a registered umbrella body since 21 January 2026.
4. If delivery is otherwise viable, [Uber's UK help](https://help.uber.com/en-GB/driving-and-delivering/article/get-your-own-bag-validated?nodeId=7eebe051-cd83-4fec-b17e-f7f53732fe50) describes validating your own compliant insulated bag. Check activation and equipment requirements in your account before spending.
5. [UK agency guidance](https://www.gov.uk/agency-workers-your-rights/fees) distinguishes work-finding fees, which ordinary recruitment agencies cannot charge, from optional paid services. Ask for terms and whether a service is required before paying. A platform verification charge is a separate question from a recruitment agency charging to place you.

If there is an immediate threat, call 999. For a past incident you can [report it to the Met online](https://www.met.police.uk/ro/report/ocr/af/how-to-report-a-crime/). The [NHS also lists sexual assault referral support](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/sexual-health/help-after-rape-and-sexual-assault/) available without a police report. These experiences do not need to become part of a job pitch.

## 6. Small quant packet

Use [Ctrl-Alt-DefeatTheMarket](https://github.com/MarkBrezina/Ctrl-Alt-DefeatTheMarket), your existing public IMC work. Give a reviewer one entry point: a notebook or script, one chart and a short note. One suitable question is whether an order-book imbalance feature adds predictive information on a held-out competition round. Use a simple baseline, state the split and timing, and show an honest result including where it fails. If you show strategy P&L, label the simulated fill/cost assumptions. Competition evidence does not imply a live return.

Keep the broader adaptive framework in your research archive. Its eventual value depends on what individual experiments establish. For this month's job search, the narrower demonstration is an achievable piece you can explain in an interview.

## 7. Review the search weekly

**First two days:** edit the first page of the reporting CV, apply to one well-matched vacancy and contact Lewisham Works if income is uncertain. Prepare one example you can explain in two minutes.

**Weeks 1–4:** aim for four or five well-matched applications a week, two focused professional messages and one interview practice block. Fit this inside your existing available hours. Start with the application deadlines; do not wait to finish a project before applying.

After about 15–20 suitable applications have had time for decisions, review where you lose opportunities. The numbers are a checkpoint, not a target response rate or a controlled experiment. If screens are scarce, get a hiring-side review of the first page and one completed application form. If you repeatedly pass tests and lose at final selection, practise role-specific examples and reconsider seniority. Keep live, unanswered applications distinct from known rejections.
