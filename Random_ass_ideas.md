## Personal productivity helper app
For stuff like domestic choirs, research projects, job application work and such. 
This should tie between my to-do list, my bigger goals, my calendar and my AI agents.
Such that I can post in tasks to do, goals that map to those to dos, map into my calendar for when they should be done.
But it should also be able to help me build an overview of when one task gets done, my goal is X% done. 
As well as map to my day to day productivity, to show me if I have increased my value or if I am being more productive today than I should be, so I need to rest or whether I have accomplished all I set out to do for that day.

Say for example I set out to do vacuum every saturday, go to the farmers market and go for a walk of 5km. If I accomplish all of those + something more, than I have essentially reached above my 100% goal for that day.
If I don't do any of those on my saturday deadline, I have reached 0% of my goal. In that way I can track whether I am getting better or worse.
